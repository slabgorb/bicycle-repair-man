<purpose>
Frame the architectural problem by gathering the inputs, scope, and stakeholders needed to proceed. Establish what decision needs to be made and why.
</purpose>

<instructions>
1. Read any provided PRD, feature brief, or problem statement.
2. Identify the decision being made and the architectural concern it addresses.
3. List known stakeholders and affected systems.
4. Write a one-paragraph problem framing that names the decision, the context, and the success criteria.
5. Confirm scope: what is in and out of scope for this architecture session.
</instructions>

<output>
A problem-framing summary covering: decision to be made, context, affected systems, stakeholders, and explicit scope boundaries.
</output>
